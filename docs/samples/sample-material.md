# The Water Cycle

Water moves around the Earth in a loop. It never really goes away. It only changes where it is and what form it takes.

## Evaporation

The sun heats water in oceans, lakes and rivers. Some of it turns into **water vapor**, a gas that rises into the air. Plants add more through *transpiration*. See [the NASA water cycle page](https://www.nasa.gov/water-cycle) for a diagram.

## Condensation

As vapor rises it cools. It turns back into tiny droplets, e.g. around specks of dust. Millions of droplets together make a cloud.

## The main stages

1. Evaporation
2. Condensation
3. Precipitation
4. Collection

## Numbers worth knowing
