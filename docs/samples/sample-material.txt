Why the sky is blue

Sunlight looks white, but it is really a mix of all the colors of the
rainbow. When sunlight enters the atmosphere, it bumps into tiny gas
molecules. Blue light is scattered in every direction much more than red
light is.

That scattered blue light reaches your eyes from all over the sky. This is
why the sky looks blue during the day. At sunrise and sunset the light has
to travel through much more air, so most of the blue is scattered away and
the sky turns orange and red.
